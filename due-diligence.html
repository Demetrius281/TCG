            06 / FAQ

            Questions Buyers Ask Before Ordering.

            Does Preliminary Due Diligence replace a full legal review?+

            TCG Preliminary Due Diligence covers many of the same areas, including zoning, building permits, land rights, company registration and business licensing. However, it is an online records-based assessment, not a formal legal opinion or a substitute for detailed title verification at the land office.

            Which areas and property types do you cover?+

            Currently focused on Badung, Bali. The service covers land plots, completed villas, unfinished properties and off-plan developments. Available checks depend on the selected plan and accessible records.

            Do I need documents to order?+

            No. A property location and the requested seller details are enough to get started. Providing a land certificate, PBG, SLF or company documents allows additional cross-checks. Any limitations will be clearly identified in the report.

            What if an official record is unavailable?+

            The finding is marked “Not Verified”, with an explanation and recommended next steps. Missing records are never treated as proof that a property is either legally compliant or non-compliant.

            How fast will I receive the report?+

            Basic reports are delivered within 12 hours, Standard within 24 hours and Comprehensive within 48 hours. Your report arrives by email with findings, identified risks and recommended next steps.

            Can I check a property before paying a deposit?+

            Yes. TCG Preliminary Due Diligence is designed to identify potential problems before transferring money, signing agreements or committing to expensive legal procedures.
