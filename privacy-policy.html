Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

01Introduction

Twins Consulting Group (“TCG”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is operated by PT Twins Consulting Group, a company established in Indonesia. PT Twins Consulting Group is responsible for the personal data described in this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website at https://twinsconsulting.group, contact us or use our professional services.

We handle personal data in accordance with applicable Indonesian law, including Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection.

Where a written engagement agreement applies, its confidentiality and data-handling terms operate alongside this Policy, subject always to mandatory applicable law and the rights of individuals under that law.

02Our Website

Our website and the services supporting it may use cookies, similar technologies, hosting logs, security tools and analytics to operate, maintain, protect and improve the website, understand usage and measure performance. Where applicable law requires consent for particular technologies, we will obtain it before using them. The technologies actually used may change as the website develops.

Certain contact links or forms may prepare a message in WhatsApp or another communication service rather than submitting it directly to TCG. Other website functions may process information entered or submitted through them. The information processed depends on the function used and the service providers involved. A message prepared in WhatsApp is not delivered to us unless you send it.

When the website is accessed, TCG and its hosting, security or analytics providers may process technical and usage information, including IP addresses, device and browser data, approximate location derived from IP, pages visited, referring URLs, timestamps, interaction events, and diagnostic information, as needed for website operations and legitimate business purposes.

03Information We Receive

We may collect or receive personal information through website technologies, direct communications, professional engagements, referrals, business partners and public or official sources where lawful and relevant. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Your name, telephone number and, if provided, email address, organisation and role.
  • The content of your messages.
  • Property information, such as location, coordinates, ownership details and development status.
  • Documents you choose to send for review, such as land certificates, lease agreements, building permits, architectural plans and supporting legal records.

We seek to limit the information used to what is relevant and reasonably necessary for the disclosed purposes, including assessing enquiries, providing services, business administration and complying with legal obligations.

Please avoid sending identity documents, banking information or other sensitive records unless relevant to a requested review or engagement. Where such records are received, they will be handled according to their nature, the applicable purpose and relevant legal requirements. Information not required for a lawful purpose may be deleted or otherwise restricted.

When providing information or documents, you should ensure they are accurate, complete to the extent relevant, and obtained and shared lawfully. TCG may rely on submitted materials in its assessments, subject to the scope and terms of the applicable engagement.

04Information About Other People

Property documents often contain personal data of other individuals, such as co-owners, landowners, sellers, tenants or contractors.

If you provide another person’s information, you represent that you have a lawful basis to disclose it and have provided any required notices or obtained any required permissions. TCG may process that information for the relevant enquiry or engagement, associated verification, legal compliance, recordkeeping and the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims, subject to applicable law.

05How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for legitimate professional purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and communicating with prospective clients.
  • Conducting preliminary property reviews.
  • Evaluating legal, licensing, zoning, construction and investment-related matters.
  • Preparing assessments, recommendations and reports.
  • Coordinating agreed professional services and communicating with relevant specialists.
  • Maintaining necessary business records, complying with applicable legal obligations and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

We process personal data only where an appropriate legal basis applies. This may include your consent, steps requested before entering into an agreement, performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, our legitimate interests or another basis permitted by Indonesian law.

We do not sell personal information as a business. Any use for direct marketing, advertising measurement or similar purposes will be subject to applicable law, relevant notices and any required consent or opt-out rights.

06Confidentiality and Disclosure

Property documents may contain confidential information relating to ownership, contractual arrangements, financial interests or development plans. We treat these materials as confidential and limit access to persons who reasonably require them for the relevant engagement.

Where appropriate and legally permitted, we may share necessary information with external professionals involved in your case, such as licensed notaries, legal advisers, architects, engineers and valuation professionals. Sharing is limited to information reasonably relevant to the purpose, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection arrangements where required.

We may disclose information to government authorities, courts or other parties where required by law, under a lawful order or where necessary for an authorised procedure.

If PT Twins Consulting Group is involved in a merger, acquisition or restructuring, personal data may be transferred to the successor entity subject to the notification and other requirements of applicable law.

We do not publicly disclose client documents or identifiable case information without your authorisation or another appropriate legal basis.

When you communicate with us through WhatsApp, your messages and attachments are also processed by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. If you contact us by email, your message is processed by the relevant email service providers.

Third-party services may process data under their own policies and, in some cases, as service providers to TCG. We are responsible for our own processing and for meeting applicable obligations when engaging processors; third-party terms do not remove those obligations.

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

08Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse. Access to client information is restricted according to operational need.

No electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will take appropriate steps and provide notifications as required by applicable law.

For verification of a communication claiming to come from TCG, use the contact details in Section 15. Be cautious about unexpected requests for payments or confidential documents.

09Data Retention

Retention depends on the type of information, the purposes of processing and applicable legal or contractual requirements. Enquiry correspondence, assessment materials, business records, technical logs and related information may be kept for differing periods as reasonably necessary to operate the website, administer engagements, meet recordkeeping obligations, resolve disputes and establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Applicable retention periods and criteria may be communicated more specifically where required by law.

Once information is no longer required for a lawful purpose, TCG will take appropriate steps regarding its deletion, destruction, anonymisation or restriction, taking account of legal retention duties and practical backup cycles.

You may ask us to delete messages and documents you have sent us. We will review such requests in accordance with Indonesian law and any overriding contractual or legal obligations. Copies held by WhatsApp or other third-party services remain subject to their own retention practices.

10International Data Transfers

Website infrastructure, communications, cloud services and professional support may involve processing or access from outside Indonesia. Such transfers are subject to applicable Indonesian requirements, including the conditions under Article 56 of Law No. 27 of 2022. We will assess and implement the required transfer basis or safeguards for the relevant transfer; this notice does not itself constitute consent where separate consent is legally required.

11Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about how your personal data is processed.
  • Access and obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion or cessation of processing where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing.
  • Object to certain processing and exercise other rights available under Indonesian personal data protection law.

These rights may be limited where the law provides exceptions, or where fulfilling a request would affect the rights of others or our legal obligations.

Withdrawing consent or requesting deletion may prevent us from continuing an assessment or service. It does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To submit a request, please contact us using the details in Section 15. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request involving personal or confidential information. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

12Age Requirement

Our professional services are intended for adults and business representatives. We do not intentionally solicit children’s personal data. If we learn that a child’s information has been processed without a required legal basis or parental or guardian permission, we will evaluate and address it in accordance with applicable law.
