05 How We Work

Choose the level of involvement that fits.

Three engagement models — from independent analysis to full partnership.

Assessment and roadmap

Fixed scope, fixed fee. A complete analysis of the land — what it's worth, which monetization paths exist, and a clear recommendation on next steps.

Involved through to execution

From strategy to close. Compensation structured in two parts: base fee and success component tied to transaction value. Interests aligned with outcome — no close, no success fee.

Long-term partnership

Equity participation from the start. TCG joins as co-owner in the project (via SPV or direct stake). Risk and return are shared. The landowner's upside is TCG's upside.
