Questions About TCG.

What does TCG do?+

TCG is a property technology company in Bali. Its platform assembles zoning, land, building and company records into one verified file for each asset. On that file we run due diligence for buyers, strategy for owners and developers, and acquisitions with partner capital.

Explore the Platform →
Where does the platform's data come from?+

From official registers: BATARA and BPN for zoning and land certificates, SIMBG for PBG and SLF, AHU and OSS for company records. TCG adds its own market data on developers, pricing and construction history. Each finding is linked to the records behind it.

Can I check a property before paying a deposit?+

Yes. Preliminary Due Diligence screens zoning, building permits, the seller's company records and the land documents you provide, with a report in as little as 12 hours. It shows the risks before you commit to a deposit or full legal due diligence.

Explore Preliminary Due Diligence →
Does TCG invest in properties it checks?+

TCG invests only in assets that pass the same checks we run for clients, and states that interest before any work begins. Assets sourced for our own or partner capital are never offered to buyer clients, which keeps advice and investment separate.

Can I invest alongside TCG?+

Yes. Partner investors co-invest with TCG in verified Bali assets, from stalled projects to bulk purchases of developer stock. Terms are agreed per deal, after the asset has passed verification.

Invest Alongside TCG →
Where does TCG work?+

Acquisitions and online due diligence focus on Bali. Advisory covers all of Indonesia, and deal structuring extends to investors in the UAE, Singapore and Hong Kong.

Can I work with TCG from abroad?+

Yes. Reports, consultations and negotiations run remotely. Where a step requires a signature in Indonesia, it is completed through a partner notary, in person or by power of attorney.
