07 / FAQ

Questions About Getting Your Property Legal

Can TCG handle the entire legalization process?+

TCG can manage an agreed implementation program, coordinating qualified specialists, documents, applications and follow-ups with the relevant authorities. Scope, fees and responsibilities are established after the initial assessment. Official approvals remain with the competent authorities.

Do I need to be in Bali?+

Not necessarily. Much of the process can be coordinated locally while you remain overseas. We provide updates, organize specialist work and identify steps requiring your personal attendance or formal authorization.

How long will it take to make my property legal?+

The initial assessment targets five business days after payment and receipt of the required documents. Implementation depends on the corrective measures, specialist work and official procedures. We establish indicative milestones before implementation; approval dates cannot be guaranteed.

What if legalization is not possible?+

We explain the restrictions and the lawful alternatives that remain available, which may include another permitted use, a revised operating structure or preparation for sale. No corrective route or approval is promised when the regulations do not support it.

Can TCG make my villa legal for short-term rentals?+

Only where the zoning, approved building function, SLF and business licensing permit the intended activity, or a lawful corrective route exists. The assessment establishes feasibility. TCG can coordinate separately contracted implementation, but cannot override zoning or guarantee approval.

NIB, OSS and Villa KBLI →
What if zoning prohibits short-term rentals?+

An NIB, business license or management company cannot override a land use prohibited by the applicable RDTR. We examine permitted alternatives and whether formal spatial confirmation is required before recommending further investment.

Zoning, RDTR and KKPR →
Can an existing building obtain PBG and SLF after construction?+

SIMBG provides an existing-building route subject to the applicable spatial, structural and technical requirements. We can coordinate the necessary assessment and specialists where feasible. Approval is not automatic.

How Existing Properties Are Legalized →
Is an IMB issued before 2021 still relevant?+

A pre-2021 IMB may remain valid for a building kept in its originally approved configuration. Extensions, alterations or changes of function may require further PBG and SLF assessment. We check the approved plans against the existing property.

PBG, SLF and Legacy IMB →
Can a PT PMA operate or manage a rental villa?+

Eligibility depends on registered activities, KBLI 2025, OSS requirements, applicable investment restrictions and the parcel’s permitted use. Current registration and licensing availability must be confirmed with the competent systems and authorities.

PT PMA and Foreign Owners →
Can TCG resolve nominee ownership risks?+

TCG can coordinate a review with qualified legal professionals and examine lawful alternatives, such as appropriate lease or eligible registered title structures. The outcome depends on the agreements, registered rights and parties involved; no restructuring is guaranteed.

What if the property has received an official warning?+

We prioritize the issuing authority, stated violation, required response and deadlines, and coordinate qualified advice where needed. Enforcement and statutory deadlines continue while an assessment is underway.

Does the $2,500 assessment include obtaining permits?+

No. The assessment provides feasibility findings, a recommended action plan and preliminary requirements. Permit applications, government charges, specialist fees and corrective work require separate agreements and pricing.

What documents are needed for the initial review?+

Start with a property location, land certificate or lease, available PBG or IMB and a brief description of the problem. Other permits, drawings and official notices can follow. Missing documents are noted in the assessment.

Can the assessment be managed remotely?+

Yes. Document review, consultations and delivery can be arranged remotely. Site inspections, local checks and authority procedures are coordinated on the ground where the agreed scope requires them.
