What Owners Ask Before Starting a Case

Timing, remote ownership, short-term rental rules and the scope of implementation

How long does the assessment take?+

Five business days from the signed agreement, payment and receipt of the required documents. Implementation timing depends on the corrections and official procedures, and the proposal sets milestones for each stage.

Do I need to be in Bali?+

Our team handles local coordination, document work and follow-ups with the authorities while you stay abroad. Where the case requires it, our engineers inspect the property on your behalf, and we flag in advance any step that needs your signature or attendance.

What happens after the free case review?+

We confirm whether TCG takes on the case. If we do, you receive an agreement and an invoice from PT Twins Consulting Group in US dollars or rupiah, and the assessment starts once payment arrives.

Can my villa operate legally as a short-term rental?+

It can when zoning, the approved building function, the SLF and business licensing all support guest accommodation. The assessment shows which of these are in place and the lawful route for any that are missing.

What if zoning restricts short-term rentals?+

Land-use rules under the RDTR take precedence over any license or management agreement. We set out the permitted alternatives, from long-term letting to a sale strategy, before you invest further.

Can an existing building obtain PBG and SLF?+

SIMBG provides a route for existing buildings, subject to spatial, structural and technical requirements. Our architects and engineers prepare the assessment and documents this route requires.

How Existing Properties Are Legalized →
Can a foreign-owned company rent out a villa?+

Eligibility depends on the KBLI 2025 activity, the investment rules for that code and the parcel’s permitted use. Our lawyers confirm the current position in OSS before any structure is set up.

Is implementation included in the assessment?+

Implementation is a separate engagement. The assessment delivers the plan, while permit applications, government charges and corrective work follow under their own agreement and quotation.
