07 / FAQ

Questions Owners Ask Before Selling.

How do you arrive at the price?+

We combine the cost to complete, the time and approvals required, remaining lease tenure and comparable unfinished and finished villas in the same area. The offer includes these figures so you can check every assumption.

Do I need to finish or repair anything before selling?+

No. We consider properties in their current condition. Clearing the site, securing the structure or resuming works before verification is generally unnecessary and may add costs the price will not recover.

Whose money funds the acquisition?+

Each deal is financed from TCG’s own capital or by partner investors who co-invest with us in Bali property. Funding for the specific transaction is confirmed before a binding purchase agreement, independently of bank financing.

How quickly can you visit the property?+

After reviewing your questionnaire and initial documents, we arrange a visit within 48 hours for properties that meet our initial criteria, subject to suitable access and any required permission from the landholder.

What if construction started without PBG or under an old IMB?+

We review existing permits, the actual structure and when works began. An older IMB may still be relevant, depending on circumstances. We assess potential compliance routes and factor the work and approval risks into the offer.

Do you buy villas where zoning restricts the use?+

We consider them where a realistic lawful use or route to compliance exists. Zoning status is reviewed during verification and reflected in the offer. Assets where no lawful use can be established are declined.

What happens to debts owed to contractors or the landlord?+

Outstanding claims and lease payments are identified during verification. Depending on the case, they may be settled from the proceeds at transfer or addressed in another agreed structure, with every obligation documented.

Can you buy if off-plan purchasers have already paid?+

Possibly. We review sale agreements and each purchaser’s rights. A structure that honours or lawfully resolves those commitments is required before we agree to acquire a project with existing purchasers.

What if my developer disappeared?+

We examine the contracts, payments and project status to establish what rights you hold. Where rights are transferable, we may offer to acquire them. Litigation against the developer requires separate legal counsel.

Can I keep a share in the project?+

Yes, subject to agreement. In a completion partnership, investor capital may fund the remaining works in return for a negotiated share while you retain an interest. The split and rights depend on the asset and transaction structure.

Am I obliged to accept your offer?+

No. The initial questionnaire, qualifying site visit and our offer have no obligation and no fee. You decide whether to proceed after reviewing the written terms.

Can the sale be arranged if I live outside Indonesia?+

Yes. Questionnaires, document review and negotiations can take place remotely. Signing and transfer are coordinated with a notary in Bali, in person or by a legally valid power of attorney where permitted.
