07 / FAQ

Questions Owners Ask Before an Audit.

Can an unfinished property in Bali still be completed legally?+

It depends on the property’s land rights, zoning, approved design, construction status and applicable permits. TCG assesses these factors to identify whether the original project may proceed or whether changes or further approvals are required. Completion cannot be guaranteed.

Does the US$5,000 audit include an on-site technical inspection?+

Yes. The audit includes an agreed site visit by a suitably qualified technical specialist to examine the existing construction, visible defects, deterioration and remaining work. Structural certification, destructive testing and other specialist investigations require separate assessment and agreement.

What if construction started without PBG or under an old IMB?+

TCG reviews the available permits, the building’s actual condition, the timing of construction and applicable requirements. An older IMB may remain relevant, but its effect depends on the project’s circumstances. An unfinished or altered building cannot be assumed to qualify for approval simply because construction already exists.

What if zoning does not allow the original project?+

TCG assesses the applicable zoning and potential alternatives, such as redesign, a different permitted use or sale. A change of design does not automatically make a restricted project permissible.

Zoning, RDTR and KKPR →
How do you estimate the cost to finish my property?+

We use the available drawings, project records, physical inspection findings and identifiable outstanding work to develop preliminary cost ranges. These are planning estimates, not fixed contractor quotations or guaranteed budgets.

Can TCG determine what my unfinished property is worth?+

The audit provides indicative market value ranges based on location, condition, applicable rights, development restrictions and relevant market comparisons. A formal valuation by a qualified licensed valuer is a separate service where required.

How do you decide whether completing or selling is better?+

TCG compares estimated additional investment, likely timeframes, potential market value and material risks against selling the asset in its current condition. The recommendation identifies assumptions and unresolved factors affecting the decision.

What if my developer disappeared or failed to complete the property?+

We examine the available contracts, land documents, payments and construction records to identify the outstanding issues. Contract enforcement, litigation or legal recovery proceedings require appropriately qualified legal professionals and are not automatically included in the audit.

Can an unfinished property receive an SLF?+

SLF concerns a building’s fitness for its intended use and cannot be assumed available for an unfinished project. TCG reviews the building’s condition and approval status to identify what must be completed or verified before any relevant certification process can proceed.

Can TCG complete or restructure the project after the audit?+

Potentially. Depending on feasibility, TCG may coordinate qualified professionals, contractors and necessary procedures under a separately agreed implementation scope. Government approvals, construction outcomes and recovery of invested capital are not guaranteed.

Can TCG help sell the property or find an investor?+

Where commercially appropriate, TCG may offer a separate sale-preparation engagement, explore potential investor interest or consider an acquisition opportunity. No buyer, investor or transaction is guaranteed.

Can the audit be arranged if I live outside Indonesia?+

Yes. The initial review, document submission, reporting and consultations can be managed remotely. The included local site inspection requires suitable property access and any necessary authorisation.
