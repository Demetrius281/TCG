01 / The Compliance Chain

Bali Property Legality: The Short Answer

A Bali property is assessed through six connected layers. Each document confirms one layer, and legal rental requires all six.

  1. 1

    LAND

    Lawful control of the property for the intended arrangement.

  2. 2

    ZONING

    The intended accommodation activity is permitted on this parcel.

  3. 3

    BUILDING

    PBG or qualifying legacy IMB, and SLF for fitness of use.

  4. 4

    BUSINESS

    The legal operator of the accommodation and its role.

  5. 5

    LICENSING

    NIB, current KBLI, risk-based licensing and supporting approvals.

  6. 6

    OPERATION

    Tourism standards, local tax and other ongoing obligations.

Each layer, its documents and what they confirm

LayerDocumentsWhat they confirm
1 — LandLand certificate, lease or other control documentThe owner or operator lawfully controls the property for the intended arrangement.
2 — ZoningRTRW, RDTR, ITBX activity rules, special provisions, KKPR where applicableThe intended accommodation activity is spatially supportable on this parcel.
3 — BuildingPBG or qualifying legacy IMB with approved plans; SLFThe building is approved for its function and the completed building is fit for use.
4 — BusinessOperator entity (individual, PT or PT PMA) and its contracts with the ownerThe legal operator of the accommodation and its role.
5 — LicensingNIB, current KBLI 2025 code, risk-based licensing, supporting approvalsThe operator is licensed for the activity it actually runs.
6 — OperationTourism business standards, local tax registration and reportingThe business stays compliant while it operates.

The working question: can this parcel, this building and this operator lawfully perform this specific short‑term accommodation activity?

02 / Overview

Is short‑term rental legal in Bali?

Yes. Short‑term accommodation is a regulated business activity in Indonesia.

Indonesian business and tourism frameworks recognize accommodation activities, including villa activity. Each property is assessed individually: the intended use must fit the spatial rules, the building must comply with the building regime, and the operator must hold the licensing for the business it actually runs.

An OTA listing, an NIB or a zoning map each confirms one part of that picture. Legal status comes from the complete compliance chain above.

03 / Zoning

Zoning, RDTR, RTRW and KKPR

Zoning confirms whether your intended activity is permitted on your specific parcel under the plan currently in force.

A zoning review identifies the legally applicable spatial plan, then checks the parcel’s subzone, the activity rules, development intensity and any special provisions affecting the site.

RTRWRencana Tata Ruang Wilayah

The broader regional spatial plan.

RDTRRencana Detail Tata Ruang

The detailed spatial plan and zoning instrument.

KKPRKesesuaian Kegiatan Pemanfaatan Ruang

Confirms that a proposed spatial-use activity conforms to the applicable spatial plan.

How ITBX classifies each activity

Bali’s official spatial-planning guidance assigns every activity in every zone one of four codes:

  • II — Permitted

    The activity is compatible with the zone.

  • TT — Limited

    The activity is allowed within defined limits.

  • BB — Conditional

    The activity is allowed once specified conditions are met.

  • XX — Prohibited

    The activity is excluded from that zone.

Each activity is checked against the zone rules.The map colour shows the zone; the ITBX table for that zone shows which activities it allows.

Development intensity: KDB, KLB, KDH, KTB

Each subzone sets its own limits on footprint, floor area, green space and basement coverage, together with building lines, setbacks, height and access. The values come from the plan in force for the parcel.

KDBKoefisien Dasar Bangunan

Building footprint and site coverage.

KLBKoefisien Lantai Bangunan

Total floor area relative to the parcel.

KDHKoefisien Dasar Hijau

Green and open-space share.

KTBKoefisien Tapak Basement

Basement site coverage where applicable.

Special spatial restrictions

Depending on the coordinates and local plan, a screening covers issues such as:

  • Coastal and shoreline controls
  • River setbacks
  • Flood, tsunami, landslide and other hazard layers
  • Protected agricultural land and protected rice fields
  • Protected forest
  • Heritage and sacred-area provisions
  • Aviation / KKOP restrictions
  • Road reservations and planned infrastructure
  • Water-catchment and other special planning provisions

Three regulations issued in 2026 tighten these controls:

In force

Perda Bali No. 4/2026

Productive land and nominee ownership · in force since 23 February 2026

Controls conversion of productive agricultural land to commercial use, including villas, and prohibits nominee land ownership. Violations carry criminal sanctions.

In force

Perda Bali No. 3/2026

Beaches and coastal setbacks

Protects beaches and coastal setback zones (sempadan pantai). Every coastal property is checked against it.

In force

Perpres No. 4/2026

Protected rice fields · issued 4 February 2026

National regulation that accelerates mapping of protected rice fields (LSD) and controls their conversion. It replaces Perpres No. 59/2019.

Parcels in more than one zone

Each zone on the parcel is assessed separately. The analysis starts from the building footprint, the proposed use and the boundary of each zoning or special-provision area.

How to check Bali zoning online

Official portals give a reliable first screening:

  • BHUMI ATR/BPNNational ATR/BPN mapping with parcel and spatial context.
  • BATARA BadungBadung spatial tools, including land-field, RDTR/RTRW and KKPR-related functions.
  • TARUBALIBali spatial-planning information, plan status and zoning material.
  • Satu Data BaliProvincial datasets and spatial layers.

Online screening shows

  • The zone and subzone of the parcel
  • The status of the applicable plan
  • Hazard and special-provision layers

Confirmed through documents and specialists

  • Certificate boundaries against the actual site — BPN measurement
  • Whether the lease permits the business model — notarial / PPAT review
  • Validity of an old IMB after alterations — SIMBG
  • Building conformity with approved plans and SLF eligibility — technical review
  • Eligibility of a foreign-investment structure — live OSS check

Kuta Selatan: the plan in force and the draft

As of 13 October 2026, Perbup Badung No. 59 of 2021 is the published detailed spatial plan for Kuta Selatan 2021–2041. Its replacement, RDTR WP Kuta Selatan 2026–2046, is in preparation: first-stage public consultation took place in late September 2026 and was published by Kuta Selatan authorities on 1 October 2026.

04 / Building

PBG, SLF and legacy IMB

PBG approves the building. SLF confirms the completed building is fit for its approved function.

PBG — Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung is the approval used under Indonesia’s post-2021 building regime for construction and building works that follow approved technical requirements.

For an existing property, the audit compares the approval with the building as it stands today:

  • Approved function
  • Footprint
  • Floor area
  • Number of floors
  • Configuration
  • Extensions
  • Major alterations
  • Approved drawings and as-built condition
PBGPersetujuan Bangunan Gedung
Confirms
Approval of the building and its technical plan
Focus
What is approved: function, design and configuration
SLFSertifikat Laik Fungsi
Confirms
Fitness of the completed building for use
Focus
The completed building and its approved function

Legacy IMB

IMB was the building permit used before PP No. 16 of 2021 introduced PBG. Official SIMBG guidance sets out two situations:

IMB issued before PP No. 16/2021 · building completed · original configuration kept IMB may remain valid

The IMB continues to cover the building as originally approved and built.

IMB issued · construction started under the new regime Apply for PBG

SIMBG guidance directs these projects to PBG.

05 / Existing Properties

How an existing property is legalized

Official SIMBG guidance provides an existing-building route that can issue PBG and SLF together once the building meets the applicable requirements.

Feasibility depends on whether:

  • the intended use is spatially supportable;
  • the building position complies with applicable restrictions;
  • the built form can satisfy intensity and technical requirements;
  • structural and technical compliance can be demonstrated;
  • deviations from required or approved conditions can be corrected;
  • the intended function can lawfully be recognized.

06 / Business Licensing

NIB, OSS and the current villa KBLI

NIB is the business identity issued through OSS. The KBLI code attached to it matches the activity the business actually runs.

The current villa code

55193Vila — KBLI 2020Legacy code
1:1
55203Aktivitas Vila — KBLI 2025Current code

Official OSS converts legacy villa code 55193 1:1 to 55203. Accommodation codes under KBLI 2025:

KBLI 2025ActivityKBLI 2020 referenceUsed for

55201Aktivitas Rumah Tinggal Sewa (Homestay)55130Pondok WisataHomestay-type short stays

The code follows the activity

Indonesian licensing classifies a property by the activity the business runs day to day, whatever label the OTA listing uses.

Tourism business standards

Permenpar No. 6 of 2025 sets the current risk-based standards for tourism businesses.

The licensing sequence:

  1. Identify the actual activity
  2. Select the current KBLI 2025 code
  3. Check the live OSS obligations for that activity and operator
  4. Meet the applicable sector and supporting requirements
07 / PT PMA and Foreign Owners

What a PT PMA can register in Bali in 2026

Since the third week of May 2026, OSS in Bali is closed to new PT PMA registrations in 18 low and medium-low risk business categories.

The Bali Provincial Government announced the measure on 22 July 2026, after approval from the Minister of Investment and Downstreaming / BKPM. It applies across the province until further policy is issued. PT PMA companies already licensed in these categories keep their licences and continue to file LKPM investment reports.

What this means for property structures

  • Hotels, real estate and other accommodationNew PT PMA registrations in these categories are closed in Bali.
  • Villa activity — KBLI 55203Listed separately in KBLI 2025. Its availability for a new PT PMA is confirmed live in OSS before a structure is set up.
  • Accommodation management — KBLI 55901KBLI 2020 code 55900 was split into several KBLI 2025 codes, including 55901. Its current status for a new PT PMA is confirmed live in OSS.
  • Existing PT PMA companiesLicensed companies continue operating and file LKPM reports.
  • Land titlesPerda Bali No. 4/2026 prohibits nominee land ownership. Every structure rests on a title or lease held in the investor’s or company’s own name.
08 / Operator and Tax

Management companies and local tax

A licensed management company covers the operator layer. The property itself carries its own zoning and building compliance.

KBLI 2025 recognizes third-party accommodation management under 55901 Aktivitas Jasa Manajemen Akomodasi. Operational compliance combines both halves:

Local tax registration

Local tax registration and reporting form their own compliance layer, alongside the national NIB.

The exact obligations depend on location and operating model. For Badung properties, the operator confirms registration, collection and reporting with the competent local authority.

11 / FAQ

Quick answers

What does an NIB cover?+

NIB is the business identity issued through OSS. Legal rental also requires zoning, building and activity-specific licensing — see the compliance chain.

Is my old IMB still valid?+

Potentially. An IMB issued before PP No. 16 of 2021 can remain relevant for a completed building in its original configuration. Legacy IMB

What is the current KBLI code for a villa?+

55203 Aktivitas Vila under KBLI 2025, converted 1:1 from legacy code 55193. KBLI codes

Can a PT PMA still register a business in Bali?+

Yes, for activities outside the 18 categories closed in OSS since May 2026. Each activity is confirmed live in OSS before setup. The 18 categories

Which Kuta Selatan plan applies today?+

Perbup Badung No. 59/2021, the RDTR for 2021–2041. The 2026–2046 replacement applies from its formal adoption. Kuta Selatan

Do Airbnb and Booking.com verify licences?+

The Ministry of Tourism is building an OSS-linked verification API with OTA partners, with launch targeted for June 2027. Enforcement cooperation with OTA partners already runs. 2026 updates
